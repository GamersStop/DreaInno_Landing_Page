Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't our current web agency just use a speed plugin?

Speed plugins often resort to artificial delay tricks (like holding back scripts until the user moves their mouse) to fabricate green scores in lab tests. This creates terrible real-world responsiveness (INP penalties) and breaks click-to-call conversions. True speed requires surgical code-level engineering.

How do you guarantee zero live downtime during the sprint?

We operate exclusively in an isolated staging sandbox. We mirror your theme, database fixtures, and frontend assets on a separate testing domain. Your live site, transactions, and ad campaigns run normally without a single second of disruption.

What technologies and CMS stacks can DreaInno fix?

We specialize across custom modern web stacks (TypeScript, Next.js, Vite, Node) as well as enterprise WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, and custom PHP/Laravel setups. Our remediation blueprints adapt seamlessly to your existing architecture.

How do we review and approve the fixes before going live?

Once our staging sprint passes automated tests and multi-device audits, we send you a private review link with side-by-side performance reports. Once you sign off, we deploy to production in minutes or submit clean Git pull requests.
