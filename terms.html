Scope of Studio Services

DreaInno provides premium design studio and software engineering capabilities across four primary domains:

  • High-Fidelity Web Design & Motion: Design systems, component libraries, typography direction, and responsive UI/UX architecture.

Statements of Work (SOW) & Sprint Agreements

All client-commissioned projects are governed by a mutually executed Statement of Work (SOW), Proposal, or Master Services Agreement (MSA). Each SOW specifies:

  • Defined functional milestones, deliverables, and technical criteria.
  • Sprint schedules, review windows, and release deadlines.
  • Budget allocations, currency, and milestone billing triggers.
  • Designated review leads from both DreaInno and the client organization.

In the event of any specific conflict between these general Terms and an executed SOW, the terms of the individual SOW shall prevail.
