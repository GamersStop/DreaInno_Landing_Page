Privacy Policy & Architectural Data Ethics

At DreaInno, we engineer software under the premise that user privacy and data sovereignty are fundamental engineering invariants. We refuse surveillance-driven architectures, third-party data broker monetization, and intrusive tracking scripts.

01

Architectural Philosophy & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the digital presence, software products, and consulting engagements operated by DreaInno Studio ("DreaInno", "we", "us", or "our"), available via https://dreainno.website and our suite of incubated ventures and public repositories.

Our Core Tenet: We collect only the minimum telemetry strictly required to conduct bilateral business communications and deliver high-performance software. We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade client or user data to third-party brokers or algorithmic ad networks.

By accessing our website, installing our browser extensions, implementing our open source packages, or submitting an architectural inquiry, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this document.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with DreaInno, we process the following limited categories of data:

  • Direct Inquiries & Project Proposals: When you submit our project contact workstation or email desk, we receive your name, business email address, selected service domain, budget tier, and project description.
  • Client Contractual Data: For executed contracts, we record organizational entity names, authorized signatory credentials, invoicing identifiers, and project specifications.
  • Technical Log Data: Basic server access logs (HTTP request headers, TLS version, and timestamp) collected ephemerally by hosting infrastructure to detect denial-of-service attempts.

We do not collect sensitive personal data, biometrics, financial account numbers, or geolocation tracking telemetry through this marketing website.

03

How We Process & Store Data

Collected information is processed exclusively for legitimate operational and engineering purposes:

  • Evaluating architectural requirements and issuing formal proposals within our < 24-hour SLA.
  • Conducting engineering sprints, codebase reviews, and ongoing software maintenance.
  • Ensuring network security, defending against automated vulnerability probing, and validating human submissions via our canvas CAPTCHA engine.

All communications and project deliverables are stored within encrypted repositories and enterprise-tier workspace infrastructure enforcing modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols.

04

Browser Extensions (Fogo & AffiScope Pro)

DreaInno is the proud creator of flagship browser extensions distributed via the official Chrome Web Store. Our extensions are designed under strict Chrome Manifest V3 sandboxing:

Zero External Telemetry Guarantee:
• Fogo Dashboard: Operates on an offline-first architecture. All user tasks, customized themes, and clock preferences are stored purely in local device memory via chrome.storage.local. Zero user activity data is uploaded to any external server.
• AffiScope Pro: Performs all DOM audits, FTC disclosure checks, and anchor inspections 100% client-side inside the active browser tab. Audited URLs and affiliate tags are never logged or transmitted externally.
05

Open Source Software & NPM Packages

Our public open-source libraries (including universal-form-validator and ai-prompt-firewall) are distributed without phone-home telemetry, analytics beacons, or hidden network listeners.

Code execution occurs strictly within the developer's runtime or client browser environment. Developers incorporating our packages retain full sovereign authority over their application data flows.

06

Cookies, Local Storage & Tracking Defenses

Unlike traditional corporate websites, dreainno.website operates without third-party tracking cookies, Meta Pixels, Google Analytics scripts, or cross-site user fingerprinting.

We utilize minimal client-side browser storage (such as sessionStorage) solely for functional interface states (such as preserving your preferred project filter or session validation tokens). You may clear this storage at any time through your browser's security settings without affecting site navigation.

07

Third-Party Relays & Infrastructure

We engage trusted, enterprise-grade technology providers strictly to fulfill operational functions:

  • Form Dispatch Service (FormSubmit.co): Facilitates encrypted relay of website form inquiries directly to our administrative inbox.
  • Code Hosting & Version Control (GitHub): Hosts open source repositories, issue trackers, and release artifacts under GitHub's standard enterprise security policies.
  • Web Font Delivery (Google Fonts): Delivers typography stylesheets optimized for performance and cached locally.

Each third-party processor is chosen for compliance with stringent data confidentiality and modern security benchmarks.

08

Your Legal Rights & Data Sovereignty

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction (including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)), DreaInno guarantees the following rights:

  • Right of Access: You may request a confirmation and copy of any personal communication records we retain.
  • Right to Erasure (Be Forgotten): You may request complete deletion of past inquiry records, subject to statutory accounting retention rules.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate or obsolete contact details.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercising your data privacy rights will never result in degraded access or differentiated service pricing.
